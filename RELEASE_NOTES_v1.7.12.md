# YumFu v1.7.12 — Ramayana & Xuanzang Worlds

## Added
- Added playable, source-grounded Ramayana epic world with seven-kāṇḍa route spine, regional tradition boundaries, original-character agency, and Indian miniature-inspired art direction.
- Added playable Great Tang Records on the Western Regions world: a historically grounded Silk Road travel adventure for original characters, with route continuity and a clear boundary between recorded material and invented side stories.
- Added save/load CLI support, daily-evolution smoke-test inclusion, world-specific reentry/evolution hooks, voice defaults, and storybook labels for both worlds.
- Marked Journey to the West and Ramayana as playable in the YumFu world overview.
- Updated English/Chinese READMEs and ClawHub-facing overview.
